FAQ

Common questions

How much is weekly pool service?

Weekly service starts at $150/month. Pools without a screen cage get dirtier faster, so they run a little more. Call or text and you'll get an exact price.

My pool is green. Can you fix it?

Yes. Green-to-cleans start at $400 and usually take a few visits. A 50% deposit gets you on the schedule, and the rest is due once it's clear. The pump has to be working, so we'll ask about that when you call.

Are chemicals included?

Yes. Chemicals are included in your monthly rate.

Do you do equipment repairs?

We don't do major equipment repairs, but we'll spot problems early and point you to someone good.

How do I pay?

You get one monthly invoice by email. Pay by card, Zelle, Venmo, check or cash.
